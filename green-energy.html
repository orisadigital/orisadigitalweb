Why It Matters

The Internet Has
a Carbon Footprint

  • The Hidden Impact

    Did You Know the Internet Is One of the World’s Largest Polluters?

    Websites feel virtual, but every one runs on servers drawing power around the clock. Every visit costs energy.

  • How the Internet Uses Energy

    Millions of Servers, Running Nonstop

    Millions of servers sit in data centers running around the clock to store and deliver everything online.

  • Cooling Uses Massive Energy

    40% of Energy Goes to Cooling

    Servers run hot. Around 40% of a data center’s energy goes to industrial cooling alone.

  • Where the Energy Comes From

    Many Data Centers Still Use Fossil-Fuel Energy

    Most draw from local grids that still lean heavily on coal and natural gas.

  • Explosive Growth

    From 500,000 to Over 8 Million Data Centers

    Roughly 500,000 in 2012, more than 8 million today — driven by websites, cloud, and streaming.

  • A Growing Carbon Footprint

    Data Centers Produce Around 2% of Global Carbon Emissions

    Roughly 2% of the world’s carbon emissions — on par with the global airline industry.

  • The Challenge Is Growing

    Internet Usage Continues to Rise

    More businesses and platforms means more computing power, and sustainable infrastructure matters more each year.

  • What You Can Do

    Build a Greener Website

    Efficient design, lighter images and code, and green hosting cut your footprint — and make the site faster.
