Why It Matters

The Internet Has
a Carbon Footprint

  • The Hidden Impact

    Did You Know the Internet Is One of the World’s Largest Polluters?

    Most people think websites are “virtual,” but every website relies on physical servers that consume electricity 24/7. Every visit to a website requires energy, contributing to global carbon emissions.

  • How the Internet Uses Energy

    Millions of Servers, Running Nonstop

    The internet is powered by millions of servers inside data centers operating around the clock, requiring enormous amounts of electricity to process, store, and deliver digital content worldwide.

  • Cooling Uses Massive Energy

    40% of Energy Goes to Cooling

    Servers generate significant heat. To prevent failures, data centers run industrial cooling continuously — around 40% of the energy they consume is spent simply keeping servers cool.

  • Where the Energy Comes From

    Many Data Centers Still Use Fossil-Fuel Energy

    Most data centers draw electricity from local power grids, many of which still rely heavily on carbon-producing sources such as coal and natural gas.

  • Explosive Growth

    From 500,000 to Over 8 Million Data Centers

    The number of data centers has grown dramatically — from roughly 500,000 in 2012 to more than 8 million today — driven by demand for websites, cloud services, streaming, and AI.

  • A Growing Carbon Footprint

    Data Centers Produce Around 2% of Global Carbon Emissions

    Data centers account for roughly 2% of the world’s carbon emissions — comparable to the global airline industry.

  • The Challenge Is Growing

    Internet Usage Continues to Rise

    As more businesses, applications, AI services, and online platforms are created, demand for computing power keeps increasing — making sustainable web infrastructure more important than ever.

  • What You Can Do

    Build a Greener Website

    Efficient design, optimised images and code, less unnecessary data transfer, and green hosting all cut your site’s environmental impact — and make it faster at the same time.
