How We Work

What You Can
Hold Us To

Not values in the abstract. Four things we do on every project — and you can check most of them before you have paid us anything.

  • See It Before You Pay

    You get a prototype designed for your business before any money changes hands. If the direction is wrong, the only thing you have spent is the time it took to tell us.

  • Built for a Phone

    Most of Sarawak will meet you on mobile data, long before they meet you in person. That is what we build for first.

  • The Price Is on the Site

    One page starts at RM1,200, an SME site at RM3,800, and the calculator adds up the rest. You know the number before you get in touch.

  • Hosted on Renewable Power

    Data centres account for roughly 2% of global carbon emissions. We host on providers running renewable power, and build lean sites that move less data to begin with.
