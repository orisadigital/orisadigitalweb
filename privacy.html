Privacy Policy

Last updated 14 August 2026

This policy explains what happens to personal data when you use orisadigital.com. It is written to match what the site actually does, which is less than most sites: we measure visits with Google Analytics and nothing else, we run no advertising or profiling, and the enquiry form does not send anything to a server.

Who we are

Orisa Digital Company (SSM registration 017269150250), of Kuching, Sarawak, Malaysia. For anything in this policy, write to hello@orisadigital.com or call +60 13 997 5304.

We handle personal data in line with Malaysia's Personal Data Protection Act 2010 (PDPA).

What the enquiry form does

The form on our contact page asks for your name, email address, phone number, the service you are interested in, and your message. It is worth being precise about where that goes: the form does not submit to us. It has no server behind it. When you press send, your browser assembles what you typed into a WhatsApp message and opens WhatsApp with it ready to go.

Nothing is transmitted until you send that message yourself, and if you close WhatsApp without sending, we never receive it. Once you do send it, we hold the message the same way we hold any other conversation with a client.

What we do with it

We use what you send us to reply to your enquiry, prepare a quotation, and carry out work you engage us for. We do not sell personal data, and we do not use it for advertising or pass it to anyone for marketing.

Analytics and cookies

We use Google Analytics 4 to understand how the site is used — which pages people read, how they arrived, roughly where in the world they are, and what kind of device they are on. We look at this in aggregate, to work out which pages are worth improving. We do not use it to build profiles of individuals, and we run no advertising or remarketing tags.

Google Analytics sets two cookies in your browser, _ga and _ga_W1SVHB9G25, which last up to two years and let it tell a returning visit from a new one. It also uses your IP address to estimate your country and city; Google Analytics 4 does not log or store the IP address itself.

Beyond those two, this site sets no cookies of its own and stores nothing in your browser's local or session storage. Our host, Cloudflare, may set a small number of strictly necessary cookies for security and bot protection, which are set by Cloudflare rather than by us.

If you would rather not be counted, you can block cookies for this site in your browser settings, use private browsing, or install Google's Analytics opt-out add-on. Nothing on the site stops working if you do.

Third parties your browser contacts

Some parts of the site load from other companies' servers. When that happens, your IP address and browser details are visible to them, as they would be for any request. We have listed all of them:

  • Cloudflare — hosts the site and serves every page. Cloudflare processes request logs, including IP addresses, for delivery and security.
  • Google Analytics — measures page views as described above, under Google's own privacy policy.
  • jsDelivr — serves the GSAP animation library.
  • unpkg — serves the Leaflet mapping library on the contact page.
  • The Green Web Foundation — serves the green hosting badge in the footer, which is fetched live so it shows this domain's current status rather than a saved copy.
  • CARTO and OpenStreetMap — serve the map tiles on the contact page.
  • WhatsApp (Meta) and Telegram — if you contact us through either, your message and profile details are handled under that company's own privacy policy, not this one.

Typefaces used to be loaded from Google Fonts. They are now served from our own domain, so viewing a page no longer discloses your visit to Google on that account — though Google Analytics, described above, does.

How long we keep things

We keep enquiry messages and project correspondence for as long as we are working together and for a reasonable period afterwards, so we can answer questions about work we have done and meet record-keeping obligations. If you would like your data removed sooner, ask us.

Your rights

Under the PDPA you may:

  • ask what personal data of yours we hold, and get a copy;
  • ask us to correct anything inaccurate or out of date;
  • withdraw consent to us processing it;
  • ask us to limit how we process it.

Write to hello@orisadigital.com and we will respond as soon as we reasonably can. We may need to confirm who you are before releasing anything.

Security

The site is served over HTTPS. We take reasonable steps to protect information you send us, though no method of transmission over the internet is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Children

This site is aimed at businesses and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal data from anyone under 18.

Changes

If this policy changes we will update the date at the top of the page. Material changes will be described here rather than made silently.
